# Onboarding Example

This example is intentionally generic and does not represent a real person.

## Required Inputs

- LinkedIn profile URL: provided by user
- Industry: B2B software
- Geography: Europe
- Primary language: English
- Current role or professional identity: product engineering leader
- Primary goal: attract relevant advisory and leadership opportunities
- Target audience: founders, product leaders, engineering leaders
- Desired positioning: practical operator with product and engineering judgment
- Content preferences: case-based lessons, trade-offs, short essays
- Posting frequency capacity: 2 posts per week
- Networking capacity: 10 targeted conversations per month
- Tone preferences: direct, analytical, low-hype
- Topics to avoid: private employer details, unverifiable revenue claims

## Open Questions

- Which achievements can be named publicly?
- Which audience matters most in the next 90 days?
- Is the user willing to publish long-form posts?
