# Profile Audit Example

This example shows output shape only.

## Facts Used

- User supplied headline, About section, and two experience summaries.
- No analytics or Featured section data was provided.

## Assumptions

- The target audience is senior operators because the user named them during onboarding.

## Strengths

- The profile has a clear operating domain.
- Experience includes credible proof points.

## Weaknesses

- The headline is role-based rather than value-based.
- The About section lists responsibilities but does not explain audience relevance.

## Optimization Suggestions

| Priority | Section | Recommendation | Evidence | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Headline | Add a specific audience and problem area. | Current headline only names a title. | Too narrow if the user has multiple active goals. |
| Medium | Featured | Add proof artifacts or writing samples. | No Featured evidence was provided. | Requires public-safe examples. |

## What Needs Verification

- Whether the missing Featured section is actually empty or just not provided.
