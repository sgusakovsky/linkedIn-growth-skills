# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Operating Rule

Build a strategy before drafting volume. Content should support positioning, audience trust, and measurable learning.

## Workflow

1. Inspect goals, positioning, audience, tone, writing samples, previous content, rejected strategies, and available analytics from shared context when available.
2. If working inside this repository, load `../../core/content-engine/content-rules.md`, `../../core/authenticity-engine/authenticity-rules.md`, and `../../templates/content-plan.md`.
3. Define content pillars tied to proof and audience problems.
4. Choose a sustainable cadence based on user capacity.
5. Include a discussion strategy for comments, replies, and relationship-building.
6. Define experiments with hypothesis, duration, metric, and review date.
7. Add anti-slop guardrails for the user's tone and domain.

## Required Output

- Facts used.
- Goals.
- Audience.
- Positioning anchor.
- Content pillars.
- Cadence.
- Discussion strategy.
- Experiment plan.
- Anti-slop guardrails.
- What needs verification.

## Guardrails

- Do not optimize for viral content alone.
- Do not recommend repetitive hooks or generic listicles.
- Do not invent case studies, lessons, or personal stories.
- Do not exceed the user's stated posting capacity.
