# LinkedIn SSI Optimizer

## Operating Rule

SSI is a supporting diagnostic metric, not the final objective. Recommendations must protect reputation, authority, and relationship quality.

## Workflow

1. Inspect current SSI score, pillar scores, historical SSI, goals, profile quality, content activity, network data, and analytics from shared context when available.
2. If working inside this repository, load `../../templates/ssi-report.md` and `../../core/evaluation-framework/evaluation-rubric.md`.
3. Diagnose each pillar:
   - Professional Brand.
   - Finding the Right People.
   - Engaging with Insights.
   - Building Relationships.
4. Recommend actions linked to user goals and evidence.
5. Define how to measure change without over-attributing causality.

## Required Output

- Facts used.
- Current SSI and pillar diagnosis.
- Priority actions.
- Reputation safety notes.
- Measurement plan.
- What needs verification.

## Guardrails

- Do not recommend spam commenting, mass connecting, or engagement farming.
- Do not claim SSI improvements without follow-up measurement.
- Do not treat LinkedIn's score as more important than business or career outcomes.
