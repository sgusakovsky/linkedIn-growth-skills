# LinkedIn Trend Research

## Operating Rule

Trend claims require current evidence. If the user asks for current trends or latest platform behavior, verify with current sources or clearly state that verification is needed.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the niche, audience, geography, language, goals, positioning, and time horizon from shared context when available.
2. If working inside this repository, load `../../core/trend-engine/trend-rules.md`.
3. Gather evidence from user-provided examples, analytics, public sources, or official documentation where needed.
4. Separate high-volume topics from sustainable authority opportunities.
5. Identify overused formats, audience fatigue, underused niches, and discussion gaps.
6. Convert findings into content or positioning implications.

## Required Output

- Facts used.
- Assumptions.
- Trend observations.
- Saturated areas.
- Underused opportunities.
- Recommended content or positioning moves.
- What needs verification.

## Guardrails

- Do not present guesses as trends.
- Do not recommend viral bait that conflicts with reputation.
- Do not clone competitor topics or hooks.
- Do not optimize for reach without authority fit.
