# LinkedIn Competitor Analysis

## Operating Rule

Extract patterns, not templates to copy. The goal is differentiation and strategic learning, not homogenization.

## Workflow

1. Collect competitor or benchmark profile evidence from the user and compare it against shared profile, positioning, audience, and goals context when available.
2. If working inside this repository, load `../../core/benchmark-engine/benchmark-rules.md` and `../../templates/competitor-analysis.md`.
3. Compare positioning, authority signals, proof density, content patterns, audience interaction, and network signals.
4. Mark observed facts separately from inferred strategy.
5. Identify white space the user can credibly own.
6. Recommend differentiation moves.

## Required Output

- Facts used.
- Profiles compared.
- Observed patterns.
- Patterns worth learning from.
- Risks to avoid.
- White space opportunities.
- Recommended differentiation.
- What needs verification.

## Guardrails

- Do not clone headlines, hooks, About sections, content formats, or personal stories.
- Do not infer private performance data.
- Do not recommend becoming more generic to match the category.
- Do not rank people without evidence.
