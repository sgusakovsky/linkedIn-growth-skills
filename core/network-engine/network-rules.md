# Network Rules

Networking recommendations should improve relationship quality, not automate volume.

## Analyze

- target audience fit;
- industry graph gaps;
- strategic communities;
- dormant valuable contacts;
- relationship depth;
- connection acceptance patterns;
- DM response quality;
- comment-to-relationship opportunities.

## Recommend

- who to connect with by category;
- why the relationship is strategically relevant;
- how to approach without spam;
- what value to offer first;
- follow-up timing and restraint;
- relationship maintenance actions.

## Forbidden

- mass DM automation;
- generic connection notes at scale;
- fake personalization;
- automated comment spam;
- scraping private data;
- pressure-based or deceptive outreach.
