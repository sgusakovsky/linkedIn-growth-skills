# Content Rules

Content should build authority over time, not produce disconnected posts.

## Content Types

- technical or professional deep dives;
- architecture or process reviews;
- trade-off analysis;
- case studies;
- failure analysis;
- opinion posts grounded in evidence;
- hiring or team observations;
- trend analysis;
- educational content;
- practical lessons;
- personal insights when authentic and relevant.

## Strategy Rules

- Tie content pillars to positioning.
- Prefer repeatable themes over repetitive formats.
- Include a mix of authority, usefulness, perspective, and relationship-building.
- Respect the user's posting capacity.
- Avoid generic motivational content unless the user has a real, specific story.

## Draft Rules

- Preserve tone from user samples.
- Use real details from notes.
- Do not invent client names, metrics, emotions, or outcomes.
- Provide options when confidence is low.
- Include a short rationale for the draft choices.
