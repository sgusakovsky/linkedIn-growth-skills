# Trend Rules

Trend work should identify sustainable authority opportunities, not viral bait.

## Analyze

- topic growth or fatigue;
- format shifts;
- under-discussed professional problems;
- recurring audience questions;
- competitor saturation;
- emerging terminology;
- evergreen topics with renewed relevance.

## Evidence

Prefer:

- user-provided competitor lists;
- public posts or screenshots supplied by the user;
- platform analytics exported by the user;
- current official or primary sources when claims involve recent platform behavior.

## Avoid

- claiming something is trending without evidence;
- chasing viral formats unrelated to the user's authority;
- recommending content because it is popular but misaligned;
- copying hooks, stories, or structures from competitors.
