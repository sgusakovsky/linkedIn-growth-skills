# Analytics Metrics

Use analytics to detect patterns and evaluate experiments. Do not optimize for vanity metrics alone.

## Outcome Metrics

- recruiter messages;
- inbound leads;
- qualified conversations;
- referral opportunities;
- partnership opportunities;
- speaking or writing invitations;
- audience fit;
- trust and authority perception.

## Supporting Metrics

- SSI delta;
- profile views;
- search appearances;
- follower growth;
- connection acceptance rate;
- DM response rate;
- comment quality;
- save/share rate when available;
- profile-to-message conversion when available.

## Review Rules

- Compare against a baseline period.
- Separate content performance from profile and network effects when possible.
- Treat small samples cautiously.
- Record experiments with a hypothesis, action, date range, and result.
- Do not claim causation from correlation unless the evidence supports it.
